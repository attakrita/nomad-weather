Support

Nomad Weather shows every temperature in both °C and °F, in the app and in its Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

Need help or found a problem? Email attakrit.claude@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Nomad Weather. Small and medium sizes are available. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add Nomad Weather to the widget area.

The widget says "Open Nomad Weather to choose a location"

The widget shows the place you last looked at in the app. Open the app once and allow location access, or search for a city, and the widget fills in.

I'm outside the US and see no weather

The National Weather Service only covers the US. Open Settings in the app (the gear icon) and choose Apple Weather, which works worldwide. The widget uses the same source.

Can I make °C the bigger number?

Yes. In Settings, choose your Primary unit. Both units are always shown; the primary one comes first and largest.

How do I save cities?

Search for a city and tap the star on its card. Saved cities appear under the search field; touch and hold one to remove it.

How often does the weather update?

The app updates each time you open it. Widgets refresh about once an hour, as iOS allows.
