Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Nomad Weather has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The developer does not collect or receive any of your data.

Nomad Weather shows the weather for your location or for cities you search.

Location

If you allow location access, Nomad Weather uses your location while the app is open to show local weather. Before your location leaves the device, it is rounded to about 1 km (0.01°). The rounded location is sent to one of these weather services, depending on the source you pick in Settings:

  • National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), a US government service. See the NWS privacy policy.
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit), run by Apple. See Apple's privacy policy.

Searched city names are turned into locations with Apple's geocoding service, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

The services receive the location only to return a forecast. Nomad Weather never sends your location to the developer.

Data stored on your device

Nomad Weather stores the last forecast, the last location, your saved cities and your Settings choices on your device, so the app and its widgets can show them. This data stays on the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Nomad Weather is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
